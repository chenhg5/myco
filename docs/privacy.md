# myco iOS App 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

适用于 myco 的 iOS App。最后更新：2026 年 11 月 19 日。
For the myco iOS app. Last updated: November 19, 2026.

## 中文

**我们不收集你的任何数据。** myco 是开源软件（[github.com/chenhg5/myco](https://github.com/chenhg5/myco)）。App 里没有统计、广告、崩溃上报或任何追踪，开发者收不到你的消息、文件、账号或使用情况。

**你的数据去了哪里：**

- **你连接的工作区。** 消息、文件、项目、网页都存在你（或你的团队）自己部署的 myco 工作区（Hub）上，除了下面说的语音服务，App 只和它通信。工作区由它的管理员负责，管理员能读到它的数据库。
- **这台设备上。** 工作区的地址和登录凭证存在系统钥匙串里；设置、聊天背景、网页自己存的少量数据存在 App 自己的存储里。删掉 App 就删掉了。
- **语音。** 只有你点了麦克风或开始语音聊天时才会听。语音转文字用 Apple 的语音识别：设备支持时只在设备上识别，不支持时由 Apple 处理（见 Apple 的隐私政策）。语音聊天用的模型和语音合成服务（比如豆包语音）是你自己在设置里填的，你说的话的文字和要读出来的文字会直接发给这些服务，用你自己的密钥；开了「多设备同步」时，这些设置和密钥也存在你的工作区上。
- **代理。** 你在设置里填了代理时，App 连工作区和语音服务都经过它。

**权限：** 相机（拍照发给 agent）、相册（存图片）、麦克风和语音识别（语音输入、语音聊天）、本地网络（连局域网里的工作区或代理），都只在你用到对应功能时才请求。

**儿童：** myco 不面向 13 岁以下的儿童。

**联系：** 有问题请在 [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/chenhg5/myco/issues) 提出。

## English

**We collect no data about you.** myco is open source ([github.com/chenhg5/myco](https://github.com/chenhg5/myco)). The app has no analytics, ads, crash reporting or tracking of any kind; its developers receive none of your messages, files, accounts or usage.

**Where your data goes:**

- **The workspace you connect to.** Messages, files, projects and pages are stored on the myco workspace (hub) that you or your team run yourselves; apart from the voice services below, the app talks only to it. The workspace is the responsibility of its administrator, who can read its database.
- **This device.** The workspace address and sign-in credential are kept in the system keychain; settings, chat backgrounds and the little data pages keep for themselves are in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them.
- **Voice.** The app listens only after you tap the microphone or start a voice chat. Speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition: on the device where the device supports it, otherwise by Apple (see Apple's privacy policy). The model and speech service a voice chat uses (for example Doubao speech) are the ones you enter in Settings: the text of what you say, and the text to be read out, go directly to those services with your own keys. With multi-device sync on, those settings and keys are also kept on your workspace.
- **Proxy.** When you set a proxy in Settings, the app reaches the workspace and voice services through it.

**Permissions:** the camera (photos to send to agents), the photo library (saving pictures), the microphone and speech recognition (dictation, voice chat), and the local network (a workspace or proxy on your network) are asked for only when you use those features.

**Children:** myco is not directed at children under 13.

**Contact:** please open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/chenhg5/myco/issues).
