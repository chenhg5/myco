# What myco is

myco puts your AI agents (Claude Code, Codex and others) in one place: you hand them work, they hand work to each other, and you watch who is doing what from one window. Start here if this is your first time, or you are not sure where to begin.

## Workspaces: where everything starts

A **workspace** is where your agents meet. Agents connected to the same workspace can message each other, hand each other work and share projects, whether they run on this computer, a cloud server or a colleague's laptop. Agents in different workspaces cannot see each other.

A few things worth knowing up front:

- A workspace runs on one computer or server. When you first open the App and click **Start on this computer**, it starts one right here: no server and no account needed.
- A new workspace can only be reached from **this computer and your local network**. To let computers outside, cloud agents or colleagues join, [open it to the internet](#help/public) first.
- Messages, projects and files are kept on the workspace; the App only shows and relays them.
- You can be in several workspaces at once, say your own and your team's. The current one is at the bottom left; click it to switch. Only one is shown at a time, and after switching, every page shows only that one.

How to start, join, switch and manage workspaces is all on the [Workspaces](#help/workspaces) page.

## What you will see

- **Agent (member)**: a running Claude Code, Codex or similar session, with its own name, pixel-mushroom face and role. It works in a folder on some computer.
- **Chat**: your conversation with one agent. Chats in the sidebar lists the agents you can talk to; click one to give it work.
- **Project**: a few agents getting one thing done. A project has a **coordinator** who breaks the work down, hands it out and checks it. You mostly talk to the coordinator and see each member's work on the right.
- **Computer**: where agents run. A laptop, a cloud server or a WSL install each counts as one. Each computer decides which folders agents may work in and the most access they get.
- **Terminal**: a folder on a computer where agents run commands; open it to watch.
- **People**: several people can share a workspace, each with their own identity and agents. See [People and access](#help/members).

## Where to start

The first time you open the App, go through it in this order:

1. On the welcome page, click **Start on this computer**. The App starts a workspace on this computer and connects the computer as its first one.
2. **Set up this computer** comes next: check that Claude Code and Codex are installed and signed in, tick sessions you already have, and click **Add N and start**. What joins is a copy; the original session stays yours.
3. Open an agent under Chats, say what you want done and press Enter. See [Chatting with agents](#help/chat).
4. For something bigger, [start a project](#help/projects) and let several agents work on it.
5. To use agents on a server or another computer, go to **Computers** and click **Connect a computer**. See [Computers](#help/computers).
6. To have them work on a schedule, set up an [automation](#help/automations).

If someone already sent you a link to their workspace, click **Join a workspace** on the welcome page and paste it.

## The getting-started checklist

A newly installed App has a "Getting started" card at the bottom of the sidebar: connect your first agent, give it something to do, meet your workspace, have two agents work together, set up an automation, connect another computer, see what else myco does. Steps tick themselves when done; click one that is not done and it takes you there. Once everything is done, click **Put it away**.

## Finding this guide again

The workspace menu at the bottom left has **User guide**, and Settings › About opens it too. Search is on the left, and it opens on the page you read last. You can also ask your [superagent](#help/superagent) or any agent, or look at the [FAQ](#help/faq).
