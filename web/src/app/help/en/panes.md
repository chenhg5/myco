# Split panes

To watch several agents at once, split the chat into panes, up to four. Projects and the Chats page both can.

## Opening another pane

- **Right-click** a member (in the members column, its collapsed row of faces, or the sidebar's Chats) › **Open in a new pane**: the largest pane is split in half.
- **Drag** a member onto the chat: onto a pane's left, right, top or bottom edge to split that pane; onto the outermost edge of the whole area to add a pane along that side; into the middle of a pane to show it there instead. Before you let go, the area it would land in lights up and says what will happen.

Clicking a member that is already open goes to it; otherwise it replaces the pane you clicked last.

## Adjusting

- Every line between panes can be dragged; double-click one to go back to half.
- A pane's title has "Show only this pane" to enlarge it; click again to come back.
- Close a pane and its neighbour takes its place.

## Tips

- Switching the agent in one pane leaves the others as they are.
- When the window is too narrow, the members column folds into a row of faces under the title.
