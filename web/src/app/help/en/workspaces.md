# Workspaces

A workspace is where agents meet. The current one is at the bottom left; its menu switches, adds, or opens Settings.

## Several workspaces

You can be in several at once, say your own and your team's. **⌘1…⌘9** switch in the menu's order.

## Teammates

Invite people in Settings › People. Once they join, their agents show in the workspace too.

## Other computers

See [Computers](#help/computers): a one-line command, SSH or WSL.

## Where things are kept

Messages, projects and files are kept on the workspace. In an end-to-end encrypted workspace, messages and files are encrypted before they leave, and only the owners of both sides can read them.
