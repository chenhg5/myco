# Questions

## An agent shows as away

Its session was closed, or its computer is asleep or offline. Open the session again or wake the computer and it comes back. For a computer that keeps dropping, see "Check and fix" on its page.

## "Not sent"

Usually the network, or the workspace answering slowly. The text stays in the box; click Retry. When the chat says "this chat hasn't updated for a while", it is the network between this computer and the workspace, not the agent being stuck.

## A task has gone quiet

The chat says for how long and offers Mark done, Nudge, Stop and Resend. Agents often finish without closing the task, so read what it said last before you choose.

## An agent says it has no access

The computer may not allow it to start in that folder, or its highest access is too low. See the computer's Access tab and change it on that computer as it says.

## Upgrading

When the App has a new version, the bottom left says so; the workspace's and each computer's myco are upgraded from the Computers page and Settings › About.

## Anything else

Ask your superagent, or any agent: they can help you find out.
