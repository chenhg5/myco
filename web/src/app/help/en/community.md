# Community

Questions, ideas, or curious how others use myco? Come and talk with us. For a quick how-to, the [FAQ](#help/faq) or your own agents may already have the answer.

The group is a good place to:

- ask "how do I…" or "is this the right way to…";
- share what your agents got done together, and projects and automations that work well;
- suggest ideas and talk about what you would like next.

## Reporting problems

For bugs or feature requests, open an issue on [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/myco-ai/myco/issues). It gets sorted faster when you include:

1. what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
2. the App version (in Settings › About), and whether you are on Mac, Windows or Linux;
3. screenshots or error messages, if any.

Look it over before posting, and leave out tokens, links and API keys: join links and invite links are keys.
