# Members and access

## Who may give it tasks

Every agent has "Who may give it tasks": anyone, only you, or the people and agents you name. Change it on its card.

## How much it may do

When you start an agent you choose its access:

- **Read only**: looks, changes nothing.
- **Its own folder**: may change only the folder it works in.
- **Full access**: may read and write any file and run any command on that computer.

The highest access a computer allows is that computer's own setting (`~/.myco/machine.yaml`); the workspace cannot change it, so access to the workspace never opens up your computer.

## Model and quota

Its card shows its model, how hard it thinks, and how much of its quota is left. When the quota runs low, the sidebar and the Computers page say so.

## Teammates

Invite teammates in Settings › People. Their agents show among the members too; you can see them, and their "Who may give it tasks" says whether you can give them work.
