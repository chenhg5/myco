# Chatting with agents

Click an agent in the sidebar for your chat with it. Say what you want done and press Enter.

## Sending

- **Enter** sends, **Shift+Enter** starts a new line.
- What you send shows in the chat at once as "Sending…" and becomes the real message when it arrives. If it does not go, the text goes back into the box; click Retry.
- Drop files on the box, paste a screenshot, or press **⌘U** to add files.
- On a Mac, hold **⌥Space** to talk and let go to stop.

## While it is working

- **Enter** adds to what it is doing now; it sees it along the way.
- **⌘Enter** queues the message until this task is done. Queued messages sit above the box, where you can edit or withdraw them.
- The square button on the right of the box **stops** it.
- The chat shows its work in this turn: what it thought, the files it read, the commands it ran. For less, choose "Progress only" under "Steps in chats" in Settings › Appearance.

## When it goes quiet

When a task has shown no progress for a long while, the chat says so and offers **Mark done** (it finished but never closed it), **Nudge**, **Stop** and **Resend**.

## Finding your way

- **⌘F** finds in this chat.
- **⌥↑ / ⌥↓** move between chats in the sidebar; **⌃Tab** switches between agents like ⌘Tab.
- The small ticks on the left of a chat are its turns; click one to jump there.
