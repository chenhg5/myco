# Apps

Apps are ready-made web tools that people and agents use together: sheets, bases, docs, slides, issue trackers, whiteboards… You open and edit them in the App, agents read and write them with commands, and both sides see the changes.

## Making one

Pick one on the Apps page, click New, name it and choose the project it goes in. The first of a kind in this workspace comes with some sample content to show how it is used; later ones start empty.

The ones you made are under "Made recently" and open in the panel on the right.

## For agents

Put an app in a project and its agents can read and write it: have one fill in a sheet with each day's progress, or turn a discussion into a doc.

## Making your own

Ask an agent: "make a weekly report app with `myco template init` and publish it to the workspace". It shows under "This workspace's apps", for your teammates too.
