# Projects and coordinators

When something needs several agents, start a project.

## Starting one

Click **+** next to Projects in the sidebar (or press **⌘N**). State the goal clearly, and add any materials (a spec, designs) so the coordinator and members know what to do from the start.

## The coordinator

Each project has a **coordinator**: tell it what you want, and it breaks it into tasks for the members, keeps track, checks the results and tells you how it went. A project's home page is your chat with its coordinator.

- On the right is the members column: what each is doing and who is waiting on you, the most recently active first. Click a member to open its chat beside.
- Work members hand each other shows in the chat as small cards; click one for the details.
- Right-click a member to make it the coordinator.

## Project notes

"Project notes", at the top right of the project's home:

- **Notes**: the brief (what every member should know: repository, branch, how to test), the memory (conventions settled while working; agents add to it), files and links.
- **Members**: who is in the project, and the computers its coordinator may start new agents on.
- **Conversations**: every conversation in the project and how it stands.

## Side by side

Drag a member beside the chat, or right-click "Open in a new pane", to watch several agents at once, up to four panes; see [Split panes](#help/panes).
